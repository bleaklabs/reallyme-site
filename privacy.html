Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Really Me is a personal security app. Privacy is central to how it is built: we collect the minimum information needed to provide the service, and nothing more.

Information we collect

  • Phone number and email address: provided by you when you create an account, used to operate and secure your account.
  • First name: provided by you, shown to people you interact with in the app.
  • Basic device information: used to keep your account secure.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not upload your contacts. Your address book stays on your device.
  • We do not record, listen to, or access your phone calls.
  • We do not sell or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or trackers.

How we use your information

We use the information above to create and secure your account, to provide the app's features, and to send transactional messages related to your account, such as confirming your email address, confirming a new sign-in, or notifying you when your account details change. We do not send marketing email.

How long we keep it

We keep account information for as long as your account is active, and security records as needed to protect the service and its users. You can contact us at any time to ask about or request deletion of your information.

Children

Really Me is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
